This is a scope match to the user's explicitly private personal service, not a public redistribution grant. Keep authenticated owner-only access and exclude provider rows from model contexts. Latest/recent SIP is different; IEX is one exchange while SIP is consolidated. Therefore UNKNOWN current plan tier does not by itself invalidate reuse of already acquired historical SIP. No new request or subscription is needed for this sprint's existing-data integration.

## Historical identity: reusable schema, unsafe inherited claims

The entity-actions inventory offers an issuer/instrument/listing/identifier separation: 33 SEC issuer entities, 34 instruments, 34 listings, 38 identifiers and 11 action examples. Canonical issuer join is `entity:sec:cik:<10-digit CIK>`; SEC facts are issuer-scoped, while price joins require instrument/listing plus provider namespace and date validity. Different GOOG/GOOGL share classes must remain distinct; a ticker rename should retain its economic instrument; delisted ATVI must not become MSFT price history.

**Do not import the old inventory's VERIFIED labels as evidence.** Producer `frc-v1/scripts/build_entity_actions_inventory.py` hardcodes all listing starts to `2000-01-01`, all identity availability dates to `2026-09-18`, defaults most exchanges to NASDAQ, and constructs evidence IDs without pinned original documents. It incorrectly attaches Taiwan `2330.TW` as a provider alias of the US TSM ADR listing. Those are material security/temporal defects. FB/META and PCLN/BKNG intervals, split ratios, GE spinoffs and ATVI events are hand-authored reference examples until their exact public source evidence and known-at times are verified. Null interval starts do not authorize unlimited historical coverage.

Safe join contract: source namespace + provider security identifier + effective interval + independently sourced known-at time → instrument → listing/venue/currency → issuer CIK. Reject ambiguous matches, invalid dates, unsupported currencies, lifecycle changes and unknown share-class/ADR ratios. Keep snapshot observation time separate from historical valid-from. Current SEC ticker snapshots can support observed current associations; they cannot prove an old ticker's interval. The existing alias evidence for HVT.A/HVT-A and WSO.B/WSO-B corroborates provider punctuation, but does not replace dated identity evidence.
